**Goal:** Turn twelve interview transcripts into a short set of evidence-backed findings that show what they add up to.

**Start with:** S `research-synthesis`. Your transcripts are raw research notes, and this skill turns them into themes and findings.

**Next, only if you need them:**
- S `persona-and-scenario-drafting` if you want the findings turned into personas or usage scenarios.
- S `problem-framing` if the findings need to become a precise problem statement before you plan anything.

**What you need to bring:** the twelve transcripts, or your notes on them. It also helps to say what the interviews set out to learn, so the synthesis can answer that question.

**Models:**
- Synthesis: a mid-tier model at `medium` effort. You could use a fast, cheap model at `low` for a first pass that pulls tagged quotes from all twelve.
- Final review of the findings: the strongest reasoning model you have, at `high`.

With a model table, I could name specific models instead of roles.

If `research-synthesis` isn't installed in your project, get it from the kit library.
