# Same-family light-path cases, 2026-10-27

Date: 2026-10-27. Artifacts under test: `issue-to-merge`, `merge-gatekeeper` (with `check-merge-readiness` and `reconcile-reviews`), and `route-work`, for the 0.11.0 independence change (PR #29).

Model: fresh Claude Sonnet subagents (the Agent tool's `sonnet` setting; the exact version was not shown to the session). Same vendor family as the author, so these runs are not independent. Each runner read only a copy of `skills/`, `agents/`, `workflows/`, and `integrations/model-routing/` made from the branch, with no `tests/`, changelog, or expected outcomes, and was told not to write or change anything. The workflow runners described what they would do and where they would stop; they did not change files or run checks. One run per case per round. The author judged the outputs against the expected outcomes in [the behavioral cases](../behavioral-cases.md).

Two rounds ran. Round 1 ran the six new or changed cases on the first commit of the branch (`e836e7e`); all six matched. An independent review of that commit ([the review](2026-10-27-pr29-review.md)) found six Minor problems, which were fixed in `9db68df`. The fixes changed the wording of the independence rule, so round 2 re-ran all six cases on the fixed text and added the two cases the review asked for. The table below is round 2. The round 1 outputs also matched and are not tabulated.

Limits: single runs, same vendor family as the author, read-only, and the author wrote both the fixtures and the expectations. The fixtures are short and clear-cut. They show the new rule is followable and the stops are found, not that a same-family review catches what a different-family review would, and not how the rule behaves on a real change. The earlier result rows for the two replaced cases ("Independence lapse escalated" in its old form and "Light path is not the same family") describe the previous behavior and are left as they were.
