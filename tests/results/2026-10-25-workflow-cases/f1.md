## Stage 5: Independent review. Stopped before it started

**Precondition check (independence)**

| Role | Party | Independent? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builder | Model family A | n/a |
| First reviewer (stage 5) | Model family A, a different version | **No** |
| Second reviewer (stage 6) | none available | **No such party** |

The workflow says builder, first reviewer, and second reviewer must be three different parties. When the parties are models, they must come from three distinct model families, and "versions, variants, or reasoning levels of one family … do not count." The Independent Code Review skill also rules out a reviewer who "shares the builder's blind spots."

That leaves two problems:
1. The only reviewer on offer fails the independence rule even for stage 5, the first review.
2. Stage 6 needs a third independent reviewer, and none exists.

**Result:** No review report was produced. The workflow says: "If independence cannot be met, the workflow stops and asks; it never weakens the rule silently." The failure rules list the same case: "Stop or escalate when: independence cannot be met." It also says never to invent a review.

- Blocker: independence cannot be met. The only other reviewer comes from the builder's model family (A), and there is no third party.
- Approvals given: none. No review of r1 exists.
- Decision needed from the requester:
