## Report

**Stages run:** Stage 1 ran in full and produced the readiness report. Stage 2 was reached: I wrote the questions, but there are no decisions because the fixture provides no owner.

**Where it stopped:** at stage 2, because three blocking gaps remain open: labels, required fields and validation, and where the error appears. The workflow says it "does not proceed on a guess for a blocking item". Its validation step before stage 3 also requires any value that affects appearance or behavior to be decided first.

**Branch:** Stage 4 is skipped because the form is one-off. Stages 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are on the path but were not reached.

**Not produced:** markup, state implementation, CSS, token mapping and the QA report. Each needs decisions or material the fixture doesn't contain: copy, a layout plan, and token contents.

**Assumptions:**
1. "States all specified" means the specification lists them. I did not assume it gives their full content, because only the error text is called out explicitly.
2. The approval status of the spec is unknown, so I listed it as a gap.
3. Gaps 1–3 are ranked blocking and gaps 4–7 risky. That ranking is my judgment from the intake skill's definitions.
