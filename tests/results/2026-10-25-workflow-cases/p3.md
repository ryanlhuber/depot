## Stage 1: Frame the question (Delivery Planner, Problem Framing)

*Note at top: the requester was not available, so the goal sentence below is **unconfirmed** and every statement is labeled.*

- **Goal (Supplied, restated):** Find out whether cafe customers will scan a QR code on the table to order.
- **Who and when (Assumed):** Customers seated at a cafe table who would otherwise order from staff or at the counter. No cafe, customer group or ordering flow was given.
- **Constraints (Supplied):** one week, one developer.
- **Preferences:** none stated.
- **Non-goals (Assumed):** a production ordering system, payments, POS integration, menu management, rollout to more than one site.
- **Success measure (Assumed):** the share of seated parties who scan and send an order through the QR flow during the test window. It is observed by counting scans and submitted orders against table occupancy. **Baseline: unknown.**
- **Confirms:** the scan-to-order rate meets or beats a threshold set before the test. **Refutes:** the rate falls below that threshold. **Not settled:** too few seated parties to judge.
- **Open questions:** What threshold counts as "people will scan"? (Requester. It decides confirm or refute.) Which cafe, and how many tables? (Requester. It sets the sample size.)

*Validation:* the question can be refuted once a threshold is set before the test. The threshold goes to the owner as a decision. It does not make the question untestable.

## Stage 3: Cut to the smallest slice (Simplification Review)

- **Keep (Essential):** a QR code per table that opens a web menu with a table ID, an order form that sends orders to staff, and scan and order counting.
- **Remove:** POS integration. *Lost:* no automatic order entry; staff key orders in by hand.
- **Defer:**
  - Payments. *Lost:* customers still pay at the counter. *Revisit when:* A1 and A2 are confirmed.
  - Accounts and order history. *Lost:* nothing for the test. *Revisit when:* repeat use matters.
  - Multi-site support. *Revisit when:* a second cafe is in scope.
- **Owner decisions:**
  - Keep staff ordering available for customers who can't or won't scan (**accessibility**).
  - Collect only a table ID and no personal data (**data protection**).
  - Leaving out payments also avoids a **security** surface. Confirm that is acceptable.

Every retained part serves A1, A2 or A4. Nothing retained depends on cut work.

## Stage 4: Break down and set the stop rule (Issue Breakdown)

| ID | Title | Size | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Show a menu when a table's QR code is scanned | Small | None |
| W2 | Send a submitted order to staff with its table ID | Medium | W1 |
| W3 | Record scans and orders per table | Small | W1 |

- **W1 done signal:** scanning a table's code opens the menu with the right table ID.
- **W2 done signal:** a test order shows up on the staff device during a dry run.
- **W3 done signal:** a count of scans and orders per table can be read out after a session.
- **Not yet sliced:** running the test at a cafe, which waits on A3 (a host cafe) and the threshold.

**Stop rule:**
- The proof of concept ends when the one-week, one-developer limit runs out, or sooner if no host cafe is confirmed.
- At the end, compare the measured rates with the threshold that was set before the test, then decide to confirm, refute, or extend once with a reason.
- The size bands are not time estimates. Nothing here commits to building the full product.

## Report

**Stages run:** all four ran in order, with the validation after each. The question had no confirming or refuting threshold. I kept it testable by requiring a threshold to be set before the test and listing it as an owner decision, rather than stopping. The limit was supplied, so stage 3 went ahead.

**Branches:** stage 3 flagged three owner decisions instead of cutting them: keeping staff ordering as a fallback (accessibility), collecting no personal data (data protection), and leaving out payments (security).

**Could not produce:**
- A numeric threshold, a baseline, or a sample size. None were supplied.
- Any time estimate. Size bands are relative only.
- Confirmation of the goal sentence, because there was no requester to ask.

**Assumptions:**
- The users are seated cafe customers.
- The non-goals (no payments, POS integration or multi-site).
- The success measure is scan-to-order rate per seated party.
- A host cafe exists (A3).
- Staff can receive orders on a device without POS integration (A4).
- Reading "people will scan" as needing both scanning and ordering (A1 and A2).
